Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Join collects, uses, and stores personal data. Join is a student project developed as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie. It is not a commercial service.

Data We Collect

When you register or use Join, we may collect the following data:

  • Your name and email address (provided during registration)
  • Your password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication – never in plain text)
  • Tasks, contacts, and project data you create within the application
  • Technical usage data (e.g. timestamps for created/updated records)

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate the Join application:

  • To authenticate you and display your personalized dashboard
  • To store and retrieve your tasks and contacts from our database
  • To assign tasks to team members within the same project

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

Data Storage

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore Database and Firebase Authentication), hosted within the European Union or the United States depending on Firebase configuration. Firebase is compliant with GDPR where applicable.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise these rights, please contact us via the email listed in the Legal Notice.

Cookies & Tracking

Join does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. Firebase may use technical cookies required for authentication sessions.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the project evolves. The date of the last update is noted below. Continued use of Join after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
